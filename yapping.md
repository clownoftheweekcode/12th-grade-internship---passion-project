### how i came up with the idea for my passion project
from the beginning i wanted to make a website, but didn't know what should be in it, what i wanted it to look like, or what i would do to make it interactive. then the idea to make the site hard to navigate came to my mind. put i still didnt know what i wanted it to look like. then i rememered how i really liked sites that were styled to look like desktops, which is how i came up with the design. i then came up with the idea to add a log in page, and originally wanted to make it an RPG to find the login. then i thought about the point and click desktop-like escape puzzle games on itch.io. I decided to do one of those to get the login.
