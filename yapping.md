### how I came up with the idea for my passion project
from the beginning I wanted to make a website, but didn't know what should be in it, what I wanted it to look like, or what I would do to make it interactive. Then the idea to make the site hard to navigate came to my mind. But I still didnt know what I wanted it to look like. then I rememered how I really liked sites that were styled to look like desktops, which is how I came up with the design. I then came up with the idea to add a log in page, and originally wanted to make it an RPG to find the login. Then I thought about the point and click desktop-like escape puzzle games on itch.io. I decided to do one of those to get the login.
